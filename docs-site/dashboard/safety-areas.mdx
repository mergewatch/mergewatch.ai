---
title: "Safety Areas"
description: "Define org-wide safety areas that every in-scope repository's advisory safety score applies, in union with each repo's own areas."
---

The **Safety Areas** tab under Settings defines areas for the advisory [safety score](/configuration/mergewatch-yml#safety-score) across the whole installation. They apply wherever the safety score is on, in addition to the `safetyScore.areas` in each repository's default-branch `.mergewatch.yml`.

## How org areas combine with repo areas

At review time, the enabled org areas whose scope includes the repository are added to the repository's own areas, and every area is matched against the changed files. **The riskiest matching level wins**, so a repository can add areas but can never weaken one defined here: if the org marks `payments/**` as `risky` and a repository marks it `safe`, the result is `risky`.

In the review comment, a matched org area is marked ` (org)`, because the repository's `.mergewatch.yml` does not show it. The `mw-safety` data line lists it under its plain name.

## Fields

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Name | At most 80 characters. Required when the area has no file patterns |
| File patterns | 1 to 20 globs, each at most 200 characters. Optional when the area has a name and notes |
| Level | `safe`, `caution` or `risky`; or no level, which makes the area guidance only |
| Notes | At most 500 characters. Tells the reviewer why the area matters |
| Enabled | Off keeps the area stored but unused |
| Applies to | All repositories, or selected ones |

An area with neither file patterns nor notes is dropped when you save. An installation can have at most **50** org areas.

## Who can edit

Organization admins can add, change and remove areas. Other members see a read-only view. Each area records who last edited it and when.

## When it is read

Org areas are read only for repositories where the safety score is on. If they cannot be read during a review, that review omits its safety section rather than scoring without them; the rest of the review is unaffected.
